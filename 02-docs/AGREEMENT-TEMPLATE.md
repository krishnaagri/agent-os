## 1. SCOPE OF SERVICES

Service Provider, Client ke Flipkart seller account ke liye following management services provide karega:

1. Product listing creation, optimization & maintenance
2. Inventory & pricing management (defined rules ke andar)
3. Order processing & dispatch SLA management
4. Returns & refunds management (defined limits ke andar)
5. Settlement reconciliation & financial reporting
6. Performance/seller score monitoring & improvement
7. Advertising management (daily budget cap: ₹____/day)
8. Daily & weekly business reports

## 2. FEE STRUCTURE

- **Commission:** Net GMV (delivered orders ka value, cancellations minus, returns included until resolved) ke **____%** — month end pe calculate, 7 din mein invoice.
- *(Optional fixed component: ₹____/month base — agar applicable)*
- Ad spend, marketplace fees, shipping — Client ke account se direct cut, Service Provider ko pass-through.

## 3. CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES

- Accurate cost prices, stock levels & timely information
- Seller Hub API access + user role provisioning (Day 1)
- Approvals within defined SLA: price changes >5%, ad budget changes >20%, returns >₹2,000, event participation — **response within 4 business hours** (non-response = status quo, no auto-action)
- Payment within 7 days of invoice

## 4. APPROVALS & AUTHORITY

- Defined "Auto" actions (SOP approval matrix ke mutabiq) bina individual approval ke ho sakte hain
- "Approval" wale actions Client ki explicit written (WhatsApp/email) permission se
- Emergency: Service Provider ko bulk-anomaly cases mein account actions FREEZE karne ki authority hai (protection), notification turant

## 5. CONFIDENTIALITY & DATA

- Dono taraf ka business data (costs, margins, strategies) strictly confidential
- Service Provider, Client ke data ko sirf defined services ke liye use karega
- API keys & credentials: Service Provider ke encrypted storage mein, quarterly rotation, agreement end pe immediate revocation
- Client ka data kisi third party ko sell/share nahi hoga

## 6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

- Listings, strategies, reports ka ownership: [Client / shared — decide karo]
- Service Provider ke tools/system/templates ka IP Service Provider ka rahega

## 7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

- Service Provider ka maximum liability: last 3 months ka total fees
- Marketplace ki taraf se hone wali policies changes, suspensions (jismein Service Provider ki negligence na ho) se liability exempt
- System downtime: reasonable efforts; critical actions manual fallback

## 8. TERM & TERMINATION

- Duration: **____ months**, auto-renewal (30 din notice se opt-out)
- Koi bhi taraf 30 din ka written notice dekar terminate kar sakti hai
- Termination pe: 7 din transition support, data handover, API access revocation

## 9. GENERAL

- Disputes: first good-faith discussion (7 din), phir [Jaipur, Rajasthan] jurisdiction
- Force majeure applicable
- Yeh agreement dono taraf ke signed copies se effective
